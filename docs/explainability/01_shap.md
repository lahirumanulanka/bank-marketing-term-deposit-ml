# SHAP: Global and Local Explanations

This report summarizes the SHAP analysis performed on the tuned XGBoost model in the notebook `notebooks/03_model_development.ipynb`.

Figures (generated by the notebook):
- `reports/figures/shap_summary_plot.png`

- `reports/figures/shap_waterfall_tn.png` (true negative example)

## Highlights

- Global importance is typically led by:
  - duration (post-call, strongest single predictor; exclude from pre-call targeting)
  - poutcome (previous campaign outcome)
  - macro indicators: euribor3m, emp.var.rate, nr.employed
  - campaign/pdays (contact intensity, recency)
  - month (seasonality)
  - contact channel and selected demographics

- Directional effects (from the colored summary plot):
  - High duration and successful poutcome push predictions upward.
  - Excessive campaign count and long pdays often push predictions downward.
  - Macro variables show regime-like behavior; some ranges increase propensity while others decrease it.

## Local case studies (waterfall plots)

- True Positive (high confidence): positive contributions from engagement signals (e.g., duration) and favorable macro/poutcome dominate negative contributions.
- True Negative (high confidence): negative contributions from stale/over-contacting and unfavorable macro dominate.

## Guidance

- Create two models/workflows: use SHAP without duration for pre-call targeting, and with duration for post-call QA and coaching.
- Use SHAP to audit individual decisions (e.g., borderline cases) and to explain outcomes to stakeholders.
